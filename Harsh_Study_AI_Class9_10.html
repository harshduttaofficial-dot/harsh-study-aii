How answers are produced

This school page uses mathematical algorithms in your browser. Fractions are exact where shown; π, square roots, trigonometry and grouped-data estimates may be rounded. Calculation time is shown for each answer.

Original language-model project

Actual training, separate evidence

A small Transformer was trained from random weights to generate algebra calculations. Qwen was fine-tuned with LoRA on specialised equation data. Their saved weights, training code, datasets and raw tests are in the full project.

Those neural-model scores concern single-variable algebra. They do not measure all 41 school tools. The school tools have separate automated maths tests.
